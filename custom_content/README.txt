MSD WINDOWS S1XLV — Project identity / 项目标识

English
The menu monitor displays the project's identity artwork supplied by S1XLV.
The original metal frame, scanlines, CRT tone and complete static transition are retained.
Clicking the monitor or its frame has no action.

保留原版金属外框、动态扫描线、显像管色调及完整雪花切换效果。
